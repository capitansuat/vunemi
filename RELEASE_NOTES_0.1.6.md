# Vunemi 0.1.6

This release adds Meetings to Vunemi for Apple Silicon Macs.

## New in this release

- Start and stop a meeting recording from the Meetings page. Vunemi records the microphone and Mac system audio separately on macOS 14.2 or later.
- Follow the transcript while recording, then get a local summary with decisions, action items and open questions using your selected model.
- Search, rename, copy, export and delete meetings. After a successful summary, the audio files move to the Trash.
- If transcription fails, Vunemi keeps the audio and offers a retry.

## Installation

Download `Vunemi-0.1.6-arm64.dmg`. The app is signed but not notarized by Apple; macOS may require **Open Anyway** on first launch. Meetings needs microphone and system audio recording permission, plus speech recognition set up in Vunemi.
