Personal assistant for Mac

Help with everyday tasks. AI runs on your Mac.

Vunemi helps with email, files, your calendar and the web. It asks before changing anything. The AI model runs on your Mac; Vunemi doesn't upload your conversations or documents to its own cloud for processing.

AI runs on your Mac

No Vunemi account or subscription. The AI model processes tasks on your Mac. If you connect Gmail, Outlook or websites, those services exchange the data needed for the task; they aren't Vunemi cloud services.

You have the last word

Every connection starts switched off. Before anything changes, Vunemi shows a card saying exactly what it will do, and waits for you. You choose how often it asks.

Mistakes can be undone

Everything Vunemi does is listed in the Activity log with an Undo button. Deleted files go to the Trash, and a sent email waits 45 seconds so you can take it back.

What it helps with

Ask in your own words. Turn on only the connections you need.

Stay on top of email

Finds mail waiting for a reply, sums up threads and writes drafts. Works with the accounts in your Mac's Mail app, Gmail, Outlook and other IMAP accounts, and never sends without your approval.

Which emails are waiting for my reply?

Plan your day

Reads your calendars and reminders, including Google or Outlook calendars added to your Mac, and adds events or to-dos when you approve.

What's on my calendar tomorrow?

Find and tidy files

Searches your Desktop, Documents and Downloads, reads documents, and moves or renames files after you approve. Every change can be undone.

Find the invoices on my Desktop and put them in one folder.

Look things up on the web

Opens pages in a browser inside Vunemi, separate from your own. It reads, compares and fills in forms, and you can press Take control at any moment.

Compare the opening hours of these three museums.

Use your Mac apps

Works with Notes, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Finder, Contacts, Messages and more, without clicking around your screen. Each app has its own switch.

Sum up the expenses in this Excel sheet.

Talk instead of typing

Dictate with a speech model that runs on your Mac: you speak, your words appear as text. Voice chat, with spoken answers, is coming in a later release.

Show me today's meetings.

Repeat tasks on a schedule

Ask for a morning summary or a weekly check. Scheduled tasks run while Vunemi is open, and they never send, delete or pay.

Every morning at 9, sum up my calendar.

Show it a picture

Attach a screenshot or a photo. Models that can see images describe them; with other models, Vunemi still reads the text in them on your Mac.

Remembers what you tell it

Tell it how you like to work, or who is who, and it keeps a note in your own words once you say yes. Each answer says which notes it was given; edit or delete them in Settings.

Remember: I want reports as PDF.

Record a meeting

Press Record to capture your microphone and the sound from your Mac. Vunemi writes a transcript and summary on this Mac; after a successful summary, it moves the audio to the Trash. Requires macOS 14.2 or later.

What Vunemi never does

At every permission level. At these steps it stops and hands over to you.

  • Type passwords, card, bank or ID numbers
  • Solve CAPTCHAs
  • Pay for anything
  • Delete anything for good

Get started in three steps

  1. Download and install

    Open the disk image and drag Vunemi into the Applications folder.

  2. Choose a model

    Vunemi suggests a model that fits your Mac's memory and downloads it. Already use LM Studio or Ollama? Connect that instead.

  3. Turn on what you need

    In Settings › Connections, switch on only what you want Vunemi to use.

Good to know

  • Results depend on the model and your Mac. A small model makes more mistakes on complex tasks.
  • For Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14 or later.
  • Tasks, including scheduled ones, run while Vunemi is open.
  • The app is signed but not yet notarized by Apple. The first time, you may need to click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Why Vunemi?

Little tasks add up.

An email waiting for a reply, a document you can’t find, a crowded calendar. Vunemi is made for those moments: a helper that does the legwork, asks before it acts, and runs its AI on your Mac.

Open source, built together

Vunemi's code is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 licence. Anyone can read it, check what it does and help make it better.
