Personal assistant for Mac

Everything Vunemi can do

Vunemi 0.1.14 in detail: how you work with it, what it can connect to, and how you stay in control.

Stay on top of email

Finds mail waiting for a reply, sums up threads and writes drafts. Works with the accounts in your Mac's Mail app, Gmail, Outlook and other IMAP accounts, and never sends without your approval.

Which emails are waiting for my reply?

Plan your day

Reads your calendars and reminders, including Google or Outlook calendars added to your Mac, and adds events or to-dos when you approve.

What's on my calendar tomorrow?

Find and tidy files

Searches your Desktop, Documents and Downloads, reads documents, and moves or renames files after you approve. Every change can be undone.

Find the invoices on my Desktop and put them in one folder.

Look things up on the web

Opens pages in a browser inside Vunemi, separate from your own. It reads, compares and fills in forms, and you can press Take control at any moment.

Compare the opening hours of these three museums.

Use your Mac apps

Works with Notes, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Finder, Contacts, Messages and more, without clicking around your screen. Each app has its own switch.

Sum up the expenses in this Excel sheet.

Talk instead of typing

Dictate with a speech model that runs on your Mac: you speak, your words appear as text. Voice chat, with spoken answers, is coming in a later release.

Show me today's meetings.

Repeat tasks on a schedule

Ask for a morning summary or a weekly check. Scheduled tasks run while Vunemi is open, and they never send, delete or pay.

Every morning at 9, sum up my calendar.

Show it a picture

Attach a screenshot or a photo. Models that can see images describe them; with other models, Vunemi still reads the text in them on your Mac.

What does the error in this screenshot mean?

Remembers what you tell it

Tell it how you like to work, or who is who, and it keeps a note in your own words once you say yes. Each answer says which notes it was given; edit or delete them in Settings.

Remember: I want reports as PDF.

Record a meeting

Press Record to capture your microphone and the sound from your Mac. Vunemi writes a transcript and summary on this Mac; after a successful summary, it moves the audio to the Trash. Requires macOS 14.2 or later.

Record this meeting.

Working with Vunemi

Plan first

For a task with several steps, Vunemi can show the plan first. Go ahead, edit the steps or cancel.

Approval cards

Every change comes as a card that says what will happen, where, and to whom.

Queue or interrupt

Type the next request while a task runs. It waits its turn, or interrupt to do it right away.

Emergency stop

⌘⇧Esc stops a running task, even when Vunemi is locked.

Picks up where it left off

If Vunemi closes mid-task, it tells you how far it got and offers to continue, asking before any step that changes or sends.

Projects

Make a folder a project: Vunemi keeps that work's files there, and every change can be undone.

Memory

Notes from your own words, saved only when you say yes. A task gets only the notes that fit it, and the answer says how many it was given.

Connections

Each connection is off until you turn it on. A connection that is off is invisible to the model: its tools can't be called at all. Many have separate switches for reading and for changing.

Files

Your Desktop, Documents and Downloads folders, nothing else.

Switches: Reading · Writing (undoable; deletes go to the Trash) · Converting (images, audio, documents)

Browser

A browser inside Vunemi's window, separate from your Chrome or Safari. Sign in once and stay signed in; press Take control at any time.

Switches: Reading pages · Clicking and typing

Flights and hotels

Searches Google Flights and Trivago and shows the options as cards. Choosing a card opens its offer beside the chat. Your route or destination, dates and traveller count go to Google or Trivago. Vunemi does not book or pay.

Switches: Google Flights · Trivago

Mail

The accounts in the Mac's Mail app (no password needed), Gmail (with an app password), other IMAP/SMTP accounts, and Outlook (with your own Microsoft Entra app ID). Outgoing mail waits 45 seconds in the Outbox. One-time codes and sign-in links are never shown to the model.

Switches: Reading · Drafts · Sending · Organising

Calendar

The calendars on your Mac, including Google or Outlook calendars added in Internet Accounts. It can't invite anyone.

Switches: Reading · Adding and changing (undoable)

Reminders

The reminders on your Mac.

Switches: Reading · Adding and changing (undoable)

Mac apps

Notes, Finder, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Music, Photos, Safari, Chrome, Contacts, Messages and other scriptable apps, without clicking the screen.

Switches: One switch per app

Shortcuts

Lists your Apple Shortcuts and runs the one you ask for.

Switches: Every run asks first

Desktop

Reads app windows and, if you allow it, clicks and types. Terminal, password managers and System Settings are always off limits.

Switches: Reading · Control

Scheduled tasks

Tasks you set up in chat that run at set times while Vunemi is open. They never send, delete or pay.

Switches: Setting up and managing

Your own connections

Add any MCP server by command or address. Its keys are kept in the Vault.

Switches: One switch per connection

Safety and control

Permission levels

Four presets, from Watch and suggest to Act on its own, plus a setting for each kind of action. Financial actions are always off.

Vault

Keys that tools use are encrypted with a key in your Mac's Keychain. The model only ever sees the name.

Activity log

Everything the agent did, kept on your Mac, with Undo.

Made by Vunemi

Files it wrote, drafts it saved, events it added and files it downloaded, with Open and Show in Finder.

Outbox

Messages waiting to be sent, and what happened to each.

App lock

Lock Vunemi with Touch ID or your Mac password.

Your data, your call

Forget one session, one note or everything. A note from memory never grants a permission, and words from pages, mail or files can never become one.

What Vunemi never does

At every permission level. At these steps it stops and hands over to you.

  • Type passwords, card, bank or ID numbers
  • Solve CAPTCHAs
  • Pay for anything
  • Delete anything for good

Runs on your Mac

Use Vunemi's built-in engine, or a model server you already have: LM Studio, Ollama or llama.cpp. You can also add a GGUF file from your Mac.

11 languages

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

Good to know

  • Results depend on the model and your Mac. A small model makes more mistakes on complex tasks.
  • For Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14 or later.
  • Tasks, including scheduled ones, run while Vunemi is open.
  • The app is signed but not yet notarized by Apple. The first time, you may need to click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
